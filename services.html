Services

Three builds. One standard: less manual work.

Each service is scoped as a focused v1 — implemented in your existing tools, designed around the workflow problems you actually face day to day.

01

Dashboards & Reporting Systems

Visibility without the Friday spreadsheet scramble.

Reporting systems that consolidate data from your existing tools into one dashboard — built around the metrics leadership and ops review each week, not a generic template.

  • Problems: fragmented data, manual weekly merges, no trusted single view
  • Built for: founders, ops leads, and teams running weekly reporting cycles
  • Outcomes: one source of truth on a defined rhythm — less prep, more action
02

Client Onboarding Systems

From signed to live — with every step owned.

Structured onboarding workflows that define stages, internal tasks, and client touchpoints — so new accounts move forward predictably instead of stalling in inboxes and side threads.

  • Problems: unclear handoffs, orphaned tasks, constant status-check messages
  • Built for: agencies, consultancies, and service businesses onboarding clients regularly
  • Outcomes: defined stages with owners — faster time-to-live, calmer client experience
03

Lead Capture & Pipeline Automation

Leads captured, routed, and followed up — by design.

Pipeline systems that move inbound leads into the right CRM stage, assign ownership, and surface follow-up — so your team sees what needs action without digging through email.

  • Problems: inconsistent routing, inbox-dependent follow-up, no pipeline visibility
  • Built for: sales-led and founder-led teams handling inbound interest
  • Outcomes: reliable capture-to-CRM flow — fewer dropped leads, a pipeline people use
